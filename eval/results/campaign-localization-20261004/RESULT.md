# Campaign compiler localization — October 4, 2026

The additional `localized_patch` profile supplies candidate-only compiler
attribution to the existing campaign repair loop. Ordinary profiles remain
available. The object certificate and controller retain authority over matches.

The adapter preserves original source coordinates. It checks source, diff,
object, captured dump and normalizer identities, measures statement deletion,
local retyping and insertion probes, and reports successful tied sites by their
actual offsets. Unsupported syntax, rejected probes and omitted work remain
unknown. Register/operand and frame differences are advisory nonlocal observations.
Every compile is logged with its parent; diagnostic stores cannot become repair
children. Independently certified deletion/retyping children are retained.
